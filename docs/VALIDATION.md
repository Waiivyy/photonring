## Shadow radius measured on the CPU render

Validation mode: disk off, uniform sky of radiance 1, no tone mapping, one sample at each pixel
centre, automatic field of view. The measured radius is $\sqrt{A/\pi}$ for the count $A$ of black
pixels (`src/cpu-renderer/shadow.ts`).

| $r_o$ (M) | Field of view (deg) | Analytic (px) | Measured (px) | Difference (px) | Relative |
| --------- | ------------------- | ------------- | ------------- | --------------- | -------- |
| 5         | 120.0               | 70.501        | 70.431        | -0.070          | -0.10 %  |
| 10        | 82.4                | 54.000        | 54.045        | 0.045           | 0.08 %   |
| 30        | 60.0                | 26.458        | 26.391        | -0.067          | -0.25 %  |
| 100       | 60.0                | 8.029         | 8.137         | 0.108           | 1.34 %   |
| 200       | 60.0                | 4.031         | 4.068         | 0.037           | 0.93 %   |

The residual is the pixel quantisation of the area count, so it is roughly constant in pixels and
large in percent for small shadows.

## Tolerance changes

- **Blackbody chromaticity at 1500 K: 0.002 to 0.005** (Stage 1). The analytic colour-matching
  fit deviates most from the CIE table in the red tail, which dominates a 1500 K spectrum; the
  measured offset is 0.0046 in x and y. All other temperatures keep 0.002.
- **sRGB continuity at the knee: $10^{-9}$ to $10^{-7}$** (Stage 1). The standard's two pieces
  meet only to about $3\times10^{-8}$ at 0.0031308, a property of the standard, not of the code.
- Two Stage 1 test assumptions were wrong rather than loose and were corrected: hue order is
  checked in XYZ because deep-red blackbodies are outside the sRGB gamut, and the tone curve
  equals 1 to f64 precision at large inputs.
